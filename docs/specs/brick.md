# Brick

Fast Linux-native CSV editor that will expand into a full spreadsheet application.

Initial product promise: open large CSV files faster and more smoothly than LibreOffice. Long-term goal: a serious Linux-native spreadsheet alternative.

Brick's full product spec is [`apps/brick/docs/SPEC.md`](../../apps/brick/docs/SPEC.md); its benchmarks are in [`apps/brick/docs/BENCHMARKS.md`](../../apps/brick/docs/BENCHMARKS.md), its decisions in [`apps/brick/docs/decisions/`](../../apps/brick/docs/decisions/), and its work queue in [`BACKLOG.md`](../../BACKLOG.md).
