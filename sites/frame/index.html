Slides, without the ribbon.

Frame is a lightweight, native presentation app for Linux. It opens PowerPoint decks, lets you edit them on the slide or as an outline, shows them full screen, and saves them back without touching what you didn't change.

Download for Linux
  • PowerPoint files.pptx decks, opened as they look, played with F5.
  • Fewer stepsA four-slide deck in 15 keys and clicks; PowerPoint's shortest documented way takes 21.
  • Only your editsEverything you didn't change is saved as it was.

Install

For your user, no sudo: the app, its menu entry, and .pptx files opening with it. Start it from your app menu or with frame. Run the command again to update.

curl -fsSL https://this-site/install.sh | sh

It needs GTK 4, which GNOME desktops have; otherwise the installer says how to get it. Or download the tarball above and check it against the published sha256. To remove it:

curl -fsSL https://this-site/install.sh | sh -s -- --uninstall
